Privacy and cookie policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit hollywithlove.com, why, and what choices you have. In short, I only use a tracking pixel if you say yes, and I never sell your information.

Who I am

This website is run by With Love Media Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 14696326), trading as Holly With Love. With Love Media Ltd is the data controller for information collected on this website. You can contact me about anything in this policy at info@hollywithlove.com.

What I collect and why

The Meta Pixel (only with your consent). If you click Accept on the cookie banner, the Meta Pixel from Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd loads on this site. It records things like the pages you visit, which offers you look at and which buttons you tap, along with technical information about your device and browser. I use this to understand which parts of my site are useful, to measure my adverts and to show my content to people likely to be interested in it. Meta also uses this information under its own privacy policy. The legal basis is your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Hosting. This site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub may log your IP address when you visit, for security and to keep the site running. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in running a secure website.

When you click through to buy or sign up. My offers, bookings and free masterclass sign ups are handled by Stan (stan.store). When you click a button that goes to Stan, any details you enter there, such as your name, email and payment details, are collected under Stan's own terms and privacy policy. Information you give me there, such as your name and email, is used to deliver what you signed up for and, where you've agreed, to send you emails. You can unsubscribe from my emails at any time using the link in every email.

When you message me. If you email me or message me on Instagram, I use what you send to reply to you and, if you become a client, to work with you.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site doesn't set any cookies of its own. If you accept, the Meta Pixel sets cookies (such as _fbp) and sends information to Meta. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage, so the banner doesn't keep popping up. If you decline, the pixel never loads.

You can change your mind at any time.

Who I share information with

Only the services named above (Meta, GitHub and Stan) and the email platform I use to send emails, and only for the purposes described here. Some of these companies process data outside the UK, and they use legal safeguards approved for international transfers. I don't sell your information to anyone.

How long I keep it

Pixel data is kept by Meta under its own retention periods. I keep email list details until you unsubscribe, and client records for as long as I need them to work with you and meet my legal and tax obligations.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of the information I hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how it's used, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Email info@hollywithlove.com and I'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If I change how I use your information, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
